* Project Overview
** Purpose
   Injury and availability management for semi-professional football
   clubs. Primary market: Balkan region semi-pro clubs, reached via
   partner-led sales.

** Design Principles
   1. *Immutable log, mutable projection.* Clinical history is
      append-only; current state is a derived convenience.
   2. *Clinical/availability separation.* Coaches see availability.
      Only medical staff see diagnoses. Enforced at data-access layer.
   3. *Two capture contexts.* Pitchside (phone, <30s, offline-tolerant)
      and post-match (desktop, complete, analytical).
   4. *Honest metrics only.* Every displayed number must be explainable
      from its inputs. Sample sizes shown where relevant.

** Non-Goals (v1)
   - Player self-service accounts
   - Guardian portal (deferred; required before U19 clubs onboard)
   - Training load / ACWR (requires player accounts)
   - Fixture management
   - Concussion protocol (deferred by decision 2026-07-30)

* Roles and Permissions
** Sensitivity Tiers
   - PUBLIC :: roster, shirt number, position
   - AVAILABILITY :: fit/unavailable, reason category, expected return,
     body region
   - CLINICAL :: diagnosis, notes, pain scores, exam findings

** Key Decisions
*** CLUB_ADMIN has no clinical access
    Rationale: not a clinician; GDPR Art. 9 lawful basis does not
    extend to squad valuation. Admins receive aggregates only.
    Positioned to clients as a confidentiality feature.
    #+BEGIN_QUOTE
    Pending: confirm with sales partner. If pushback, offer
    break-glass flow (medical lead approves, time-boxed, audited).
    #+END_QUOTE

*** Coaches: write-only into clinical records
    May create QUICK injury logs; may not read clinical detail.
    Serves pitchside capture when physio unavailable.

*** Rehab stage gating
    Stages 1-4: MEDICAL_STAFF. Stages 5-6: MEDICAL_LEAD only.

** Notable Modeling Decisions
*** Shirt number on squad_memberships, not players
    Season- and team-scoped fact. Player may hold different numbers
    across squads.

*** Availability as multi-cause
    ~availability_blocks~ covers INJURY | ILLNESS | SUSPENSION |
    PERSONAL | INTERNATIONAL_DUTY | NOT_REGISTERED. Coach queries
    never touch ~injury_episodes~.

*** Two severity scales
    - ~severity_grade~ (1-4) :: tissue damage, assessed once
    - ~pain_assessments~ (0-10) :: pain/function, measured repeatedly
    These are different constructs. Do not merge.

*** Expected vs actual return dates
    Four columns retained deliberately. Difference yields estimate
    accuracy metric. Training return ≠ match return.

*** body_map_hint is a hint
    ~region~ + ~side~ are canonical. Coordinates are for rendering
    only and must never drive analytics grouping.
